Scroll to explore

We back the outcome, not the paperwork. If a village cooperative, a dive shop, a university lab or a five-person NGO is the right hands for a stretch of coast, that is who we want to fund.

Too much conservation money can only travel to a certain kind of organisation — slowly, across borders, through people who have never stood on the beach in question. Meanwhile the crew who could have fixed it this season gives up and takes other work.

Why we exist →

A coastline is only protected for as long as the people living on it are able to protect it. Livelihoods, teaching, research and training aren't side projects for us — they are the work itself.

Based in Singapore. Working wherever there is saltwater and someone worth backing. We wrote all of our governance down before we raised a single cent.

The work

Five places to start

These are the coasts and questions our first funding will go to — chosen because the people there are ready and the window is closing.

Asia-Pacific reef systems

Coral Reef Restoration

Nurseries and reef rehabilitation delivered by dive operators, universities and community groups already in the water.

Coastal Southeast Asia and beyond

Mangrove Reforestation

Community-led replanting and long-term stewardship, where storm protection, fisheries and blue carbon come from the same hectare.

Small-scale coastal fisheries worldwide

Community-Based Fisheries

Locally-managed fisheries and MPA co-management — protection that pays the people who enforce it.

Global, Indo-Pacific priority

Marine Research & Monitoring

The unglamorous baseline monitoring nobody else funds — because grantmaking is only as good as the evidence behind it.

Coastal schools and fishing villages

Ocean Education

Getting the next generation into the water with a mask on — because nobody protects a reef they have never seen.
